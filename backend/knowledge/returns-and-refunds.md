# Returns & Refunds

## Return window
Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. After 30 days we issue
store credit only, at our discretion.

## Item condition
Returned items must be unused, in original packaging, with tags attached. Items that are
worn, washed, damaged by the customer, or missing parts may be refused or refunded partially.

## Non-returnable items
Gift cards, downloadable products, and final-sale items (marked "Final Sale") cannot be
returned or refunded.

## How to start a return
Ask support to start a return for your order number. We email a prepaid return label.
Standard return shipping is free; expedited return shipping is not refunded.

## Refund timing and method
Once we receive and inspect the item, refunds are issued to the original payment method
within 5–10 business days. The exact timing depends on your bank or card issuer. Refunds
are processed through Stripe; you do not need to share card details with support.

## Partial and order-level refunds
We can refund a full order or a partial amount (for example, one damaged item in a larger
order). Refunds cannot exceed the original amount paid.

## Exchanges
We do not process direct exchanges. Return the original item for a refund and place a new
order for the replacement.
