# Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I check my order status?
Give support your order number and we'll look up its current status (paid, shipped,
delivered, refunded) and tracking if available.

## What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit and debit cards through Stripe. Card details are entered on
Stripe's secure checkout — support never sees or stores your full card number.

## Is my payment information safe?
Yes. Payments and refunds are handled by Stripe. We store only an order record and a
reference to the Stripe transaction, never your raw card data.

## Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?
If the order has not shipped yet, contact support quickly and we'll try to change or cancel
it. Once shipped, treat it as a return.

## Do you offer discounts?
Standard shipping is free on orders over $50. Promotional codes, when active, are entered at
checkout.

## How do I contact a human?
This assistant handles most questions. If you need a person, ask to escalate and we'll route
you to the support team with your order details attached.

## What are your support hours?
The assistant is available 24/7. Human support replies during business hours, Monday to
Friday, 9am–5pm.
