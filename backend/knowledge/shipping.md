# Shipping & Delivery

## Processing time
Orders are processed within 1 business day. Orders placed after 2pm local time, or on
weekends and holidays, begin processing the next business day.

## Shipping methods and estimates
- **Standard** (free on orders over $50): 3–5 business days.
- **Expedited** ($9.99): 2 business days.
- **Overnight** ($24.99): next business day if ordered before 2pm.

Estimates are business days (Mon–Fri) and do not include the processing day.

## Tracking
A tracking number is emailed when your order ships. You can also see live status by asking
support for your order status — we look it up by order number.

## Where we ship
We ship within the United States, including Alaska, Hawaii, and PO boxes. We do not
currently ship internationally.

## Lost or delayed packages
If tracking shows no movement for 5+ business days, contact support. Packages marked
"delivered" but not received: check with neighbors and your mailroom first, then contact us
within 7 days so we can open a carrier claim.
