# Subscriptions & Billing

## How subscriptions work
A subscription bills automatically on a recurring cycle (monthly or yearly, depending on the
plan) using your saved payment method. Billing is handled securely through Stripe.

## Billing cycle and renewal
Your plan renews on the same date each cycle. We email a receipt after each successful
charge. If a charge fails, we retry over the next few days and email you; if it keeps
failing, the subscription is paused.

## Changing a plan
You can upgrade or downgrade a plan. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated;
downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

## Cancelling
You can cancel anytime. Cancellation stops future charges. You keep access until the end of
the period you already paid for — we do not refund the unused portion of the current cycle.

## Refunds on subscriptions
The current period is generally non-refundable once charged. If you were charged in error or
right after cancelling, contact support and we can review a refund through Stripe.

## Updating payment method
Ask support to update the card on file, or do it from your account. A new card is used on the
next billing attempt.
